Most AI research tools give you one model's perspective and call it comprehensive.

What happens when you run 4 models with genuinely different training data, different architectures, and different blind spots on the same research question -- then fuse their outputs into a single report?

We built a pipeline to find out.

Starting from Stanford's STORM methodology (NAACL 2024), we made one architectural change that transforms the quality of research output: instead of one model roleplaying multiple perspectives, each perspective gets 4 models running in parallel. A judge synthesizes all 4 outputs. A moderator catches what everyone missed.

All 4 analysis models run free. You only pay for synthesis.

Based on: Shao et al., Stanford Oval Lab, NAACL 2024. Co-STORM, EMNLP 2024.

Use it for:
- Academic literature synthesis
- Competitive intelligence
- Technical documentation
- Any research task where single-model bias is a liability
